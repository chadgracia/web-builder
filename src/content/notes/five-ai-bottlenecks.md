*Private company notes · August 2026*

While AI continues to advance at a rapid pace, the real-world infrastructure supporting it is hitting practical limits.

Building and running advanced AI now faces major real-world hurdles: local power grids cannot supply enough electricity, microchips are generating too much heat, general-purpose chips are becoming too expensive for routine tasks, training systems are running out of high-quality human data, and software still lacks physical hands to do manual work.

Here are five companies we cover that are developing solutions to these operational bottlenecks.

### 1. Panthalassa

**The Chokepoint: Power Grid & Water Cooling Limits.** Modern data centers require massive amounts of continuous power and water for cooling, leading to multi-year utility delays and local grid congestion.

**The Solution:** [Panthalassa](/panthalassa/) builds modular, ocean-based power platforms that use wave energy to power off-grid computing pods, utilizing deep ocean water for natural heat dissipation.

**Company Stage:** Growth Stage / Series B ($140M Series B closed).

**Company Website:** [panthalassa.com](https://panthalassa.com)

### 2. Lightmatter

**The Chokepoint: Chip-to-Chip Wiring and Heat.** In large AI server clusters, transmitting data between thousands of microchips over traditional copper wires generates excessive heat and slows down processing speeds.

**The Solution:** [Lightmatter](/lightmatter/) develops silicon photonics systems that route data between processors using pulses of light instead of electricity, significantly reducing heat and latency.

**Company Stage:** Late Stage ($5.37B valuation benchmark).

**Company Website:** [lightmatter.co](https://lightmatter.co)

[View the Lightmatter deal &rarr;](https://trades.graciagroup.com/deal/55260837)

### 3. Positron AI

**The Chokepoint: GPU Cost and Memory Limits.** AI models running on standard graphics chips (GPUs) consume high levels of electricity and face memory bottlenecks.

**The Solution:** Positron designs specialized hardware built specifically for running transformer-based AI models, lowering the ongoing cost and energy required per user interaction.

**Company Stage:** Commercial Stage ($4.5B valuation benchmark).

**Company Website:** [positron.ai](https://www.positron.ai)

[View the Positron AI deal &rarr;](https://trades.graciagroup.com/deal/55284645)

### 4. Mercor

**The Chokepoint: High-Level Human Expertise.** AI models have largely exhausted general web text and now require specialized, professional human knowledge to improve reasoning in complex fields.

**The Solution:** [Mercor](/mercor/) operates an AI-assisted hiring and evaluation network that matches domain specialists (such as doctors, lawyers, and engineers) with AI labs to generate advanced training data.

**Company Stage:** Growth Stage ($10B valuation benchmark).

**Company Website:** [mercor.com](https://mercor.com)

[View the Mercor deal &rarr;](https://trades.graciagroup.com/deal/55392677)

### 5. 1X Technologies

**The Chokepoint: Physical Labor.** Digital AI models operate solely on screens and cannot physically move inventory, package goods, or handle dynamic industrial labor.

**The Solution:** 1X builds humanoid and wheeled androids designed with safe, human-like actuators to work alongside human staff in warehouses and commercial settings.

**Company Stage:** Commercial Deployment (Series B).

**Company Website:** [1x.tech](https://www.1x.tech)
